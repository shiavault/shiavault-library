Chapter 95: Consuming Any Clay Is Unlawful Except The Clay Of Imam Al-Husayn's ('A) Grave; For Indeed, It Is A Cure
===================================================================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Eating any clay is unlawful
like eating pork. If anyone eats any clay which leads to his death, I
will not pray on him, except the clay of Imam Al-Husayn's ('a) grave,
for indeed, it contains the cure for every disease. But one who eats it
to satisfy a craving, will not find any cure in it.

**Tradition 2:** Similar to no. 1.

**Tradition 3:** Imam Al-Baqir or Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Indeed,
Allah, the blessed and exalted, created Adam from clay. Hence, He
forbade mankind from eating clay.

I (narrator) asked, “What about the clay of Imam Al-Husayn's ('a)
grave?”

Imam ('a) replied, “It is unlawful for people to eat their own flesh but
is it permissible for them to eat our flesh?! It is permissible to eat a
very little amount of it, as much as a pea.”

**Tradition 4:** Same as no. 1.

**Tradition 5:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Anyone who sells the clay of
Imam Al-Husayn's ('a) grave has sold and bought the flesh of Husayn
('a).
