Chapter 72: Reward Of The Ziyarah Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A) On Fifteenth Shaban
============================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: On fifteenth Shaban, an
announcer announces from the highest horizon: O visitors of Imam
Al-Husayn ('a), return forgiven and your rewards will be given by Allah,
your Lord and by Muhammad, your Prophet.

**Tradition 2:** Imam As-Sajjad ('a) said: One who would like a hundred
and twenty four thousand prophets to shake hands with him, should
perform the *Ziyarah* of the Imam Al-Husayn ('a) on fifteenth Shaban;
because the souls of the prophets ('a), including the five ‘Ul al-Azm
messengers, ask permission from Allah for his *Ziyarah* and they will be
granted it.

We asked, “Who are the five ‘Ul al-Azm (prophets)?” Imam ('a) replied,
“Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa, Isa and Muhammad.”

We asked, “What does ‘Ul al-Azm mean?”

Imam ('a) replied, “Prophets sent to all jinns and mankind.”

**Tradition 3:** Same as no. 1.

**Tradition 4:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Sins will be forgiven of one
who performs the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) on fifteenth Shaban
for three consecutive years.

**Tradition 5:** Imam Al-Baqir ('a) said: Sins of one who performs the
*Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) on fifteenth Shaban will be forgiven
and no sins will be recorded for him in that year. And if he performs
the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) again on fifteenth Shaban the
following year, the Almighty Allah will forgive all his sins.

**Tradition 6:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Allah will forgive all the
past and future sins of one who performs the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn
('a) on one of the three eves.

I asked, “May I be sacrificed on you, what are the three eves?”

Imam ('a) replied, “Eve of ‘Eid al-Fitr, eve of ‘Eid al-Adha and eve of
fifteenth Shaban.”

**Tradition 7:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Allah will record a thousand
accepted Hajj and a thousand accepted Umrahs and will grant a thousand
requests pertaining to his life and Hereafter for one who performs the
*Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) on the eve of fifteenth Shaban, on the
eve of ‘Eid al-Fitr and on night of Arafah, all in the same year.

**Tradition 8:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Allah will appoint two angels
on one who spends the eve of fifteenth Shaban in Karbala and who do the
following:

Recite Surah Tauhid a thousand times, seek forgiveness from Allah a
thousand times, praise Allah a thousand times and then perform four
Rakats prayer reciting Ayat ul-Kursi a thousand times in each Rakat
after Surah Hamd.

The two angels will protect him from any harm and from all the evil of
every Shaitan and every ruler. They will only record their good deeds
and no sin will be recorded for him. The two angels will continuously
seek forgiveness for him as long as they are with them.

**Tradition 9:** Same point as mentioned in no. 6.

**Tradition 10:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: O Yunus, Allah will forgive
all past and future sins of the believer who performs the *Ziyarah* of
Imam Al-Husayn ('a) on the eve of fifteenth Shaban and he will be told,
“Continue with good deeds.”

I said, “All of this will be for one who performs the *Ziyarah* of Imam
Al-Husayn ('a) on fifteenth Shaban?”

Imam ('a) replied, “O Yunus, if I tell people about that which will be
given to one who performs the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) on this
night, they would travel riding on a piece of wood placed on the back of
saddleless camels to go to him.”

**Tradition 11:** Same points as mentioned before.
