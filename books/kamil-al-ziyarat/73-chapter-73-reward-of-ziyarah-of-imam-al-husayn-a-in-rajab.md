Chapter 73: Reward Of Ziyarah Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A) In Rajab
=============================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam Ar-Ridha’ ('a) was asked, “In which month should
we perform the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a)?” Imam ('a) replied, “On
fifteenth Rajab and fifteenth Shaban.”

**Tradition 2:** Imam 'As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Allah will record the reward
of a thousand Hajj, a thousand Umrahs and fighting a thousand Jihads
with a divine messenger for one who performs the *Ziyarah* of Imam
Husayn ('a) on Arafah Day with recognition of his rights. And Allah
forgives one who goes to his *Ziyarah* on first Rajab.
