Chapter 62: Ziyarah Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A) Erases Sins
======================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Visitor of Imam Al-Husayn ('a)
places his sins at the door of his house like a bridge and leaves them
behind, crossing it in the same way as one leaves a bridge behind after
crossing it.

**Tradition 2:** Same points as mentioned before.

**Tradition 3:** Same as Chapter 49, Tradition no.3.

**Tradition 4:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: When you intend to return from
the grave of Imam Al-Husayn ('a), an announcer will announce to you. If
you heard that call, you would stay there for the rest of your life.

The announcer will say, “Glad tidings to you, O servant of Allah! Surely
you have succeeded, and you are safe. Surely your past sins have been
forgiven. So, continue with good deeds.”

**Tradition 5:** Imam Al-Kadhim ('a) said: The least reward for one who
performs the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) by the banks of Euphrates
with recognition of his rights and believing in his sanctity and Wilayat
is that his past and future sins will be forgiven.

**Tradition 6:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: One who wants to be honoured
by Allah and be included in the intercession of Muhammad on Judgment Day
should perform the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a). The Almighty Allah
will honour him with the best honour and rewards and not question him
for any sins committed during his life, even if they are as numerous as
grains of sand or as great as mountains of Tahamah or as much as foam in
the seas. When Husayn Ibn Ali ('a) was martyred:

Firstly: He was oppressed

Secondly: He was dealt with unjustly

Thirdly: He, his family and companions were thirsty.

**Tradition 7:** Imam Al-Kadhim ('a) said: When one sets out to perform
the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a), Allah will appoint an angel on him
who will place his finger on his back and will begin writing each word
that comes out of his mouth till he arrives at the shrine.

After he exits the shrine gates, the angel will place his palm on the
middle of his back and says, “All your past sins have been forgiven; so
continue with the good deeds.”

**Tradition 8:** Imam As-Sajjad ('a) said: Allah will forgive the sins
of one who performs the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) for the sake of
divine pleasure. He will become sinless like a newborn child. Angels
will accompany them all way and spread their wings over them till they
return home. Angels will seek forgiveness for them and call out to them,
“You are blessed and blessed is one whose *Ziyarah* you performed.” He
will be continuously protected along with his kin.
