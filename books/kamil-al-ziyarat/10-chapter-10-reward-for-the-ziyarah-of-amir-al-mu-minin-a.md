Chapter 10: Reward For The Ziyarah Of Amir Al-Mu’minin ('A)
===========================================================

**Tradition 1:** Abu Wahab Basri says: I entered Medina, went to Imam
As-Sadiq (‘a) and said, “May I be sacrificed on you, I have come to you,
but I have not gone to the Ziyarah of the grave of Amir Al-Mu’minin
('a).”

Imam ('a) replied, “Very bad! If you were not one of our Shia, I would
not even have glanced at you. Do you not perform the *Ziyarah* of one to
whose *Ziyarah* the Almighty Allah, along with His angels goes, and to
which prophets and believers go?”

“May Ibe sacrificed on you! I did not know this,” I said. ''Then know
that Amir Al-Mu’minin ('a) has a higher rank with Allah than all Imams
('a). Imams ('a) are above others due to the number of their deeds, and
the reward for their deeds is also for Amir Al-Mu’minin ('a).

**Tradition 2:** Mufaddal Ibn Umar said: I went to Imam As-Sadiq (‘a)
and said, “I yearn to go to Ghari!”

“Why?” asked the Imam ('a).

“Because I love Amir Al-Mu’minin ('a) and would like to go for his
*Ziyarah*,”

“Do you know the excellence of his *Ziyarah*?”

“No, O son of Allah's Messenger! Tell me about it.” “When you intend to
perform the *Ziyarah* of Amir Al-Mu’minin ('a), know that you will do
the *Ziyarah* of the bones of Adam ('a), body of Nuh ('a), and the body
of Amir Al-Mu’minin ('a).”

I asked, “Adam ('a) landed in Sarandib at the place of the rising of the
Sun, and it is commonly believed that his bones were buried in Ka’aba.
So how can you say that he is in Kufa?”

Imam ('a) replied: When Nuh ('a) was in the ark, Almighty Allah revealed
to him to go circle the Ka’aba seven times. Nuh ('a) complied. Then he
descended from the ark, went into the knee-deep water till he brought
out a coffin containing the bones of Adam ('a). He brought it to his ark
and continued circling the Ka’aba as long as Allah had willed. Then he
moved to Kufa, went inside Kufa Masjid with his ark and stopped in the
middle. This is where Allah revealed to the earth,

> وَقِيلَ يَا أَرْضُ ابْلَعِي مَاءَكِ

***“Swallow your water” (11:44).***

So, the earth swallowed its water beginning from Kufa Masjid, from where
the deluge had originally begun. Then those who were with Nuh ('a) in
the ark each went in different directions. Nuh ('a) carried the coffin
and buried it in Ghari which is the part of the mountain on which Allah
spoke to Musa ('a), purified Isa ('a), chose Ibrahim ('a) as his friend,
and chose Muhammad (S) as His beloved. Allah chose that land as the
abode of prophets.

By Allah, no one more honourable than Amir Al-Mu’minin ('a) has ever
lived there after his purified ancestors, Adam and Nuh ('a).

So, when you intend to go to Najaf, perform the *Ziyarah* of the bones
of Adam ('a), body of Nuh ('a) and the body of Ali Ibn Abi Talib ('a).
You will have gone to the *Ziyarah* of the first ancestor of His
Eminence, Muhammad (S) who is the last prophet, and of Ali ('a), chief
of successors. You should know that the doors of heavens open for the
visitor of His Eminence, so do not be ignorant of this great blessing.

**Tradition 3:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Imam Al-Hasan ('a) asked
Allah's Messenger (S), “Father, what is the reward for one who performs
your *Ziyarah*?” ·

The Prophet replied, “My son, on Judgment Day, it will be my divine
obligation to visit one who performs my *Ziyarah* during my lifetime or
after my passing away, or performs the *Ziyarah* of your father, and to
save him from his sins.”
