Chapter 20: Knowledge Of The Angels About The Martyrdom Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A)
==============================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: When Imam Al-Husayn ('a) was
born, Allah ordered Jibra’il to descend to the earth with a thousand
angels to congratulate Allah's Messenger (S) on behalf of Allah and
himself.

On his way, Jibra’il descended on an island where an angel, Futrus
lived. Futrus was a carrier of the Throne.

Once he had delayed carrying out an order of Allah so his wings were
broken and he was thrown on this island. He continued worshipping Allah
on that island for six hundred years, after which Imam Al-Husayn ('a)
was born.

Futrus asked Jibra’il, “Where are you going?”

“Allah has blessed His Eminence, Muhammad (S) with a blessing and I have
been sent to congratulate him on behalf of Allah and myself.”

“Please Jibra’il! Take me with you. Perhaps the Prophet will intercede
for me.”

So, Jibra’il carried Futrus and when they arrived, Jibra’il
congratulated the Prophet on behalf of Allah and himself, and then
informed him about Futrus. Allah's Messenger (S) told Jibra’il to bring
Futrus inside.

After Futrus was brought inside, he informed the Prophet of his
situation, who prayed for him and said, “Rub your wings on this newborn
child and you will revert to your original position.”

Futrus rubbed his wings on Imam Al-Husayn ('a) and began ascending the
heavens.

As he was ascending, he said, “O Allah's Messenger (S)! Indeed, your
Ummah will soon kill him. Indeed, as my recompense to him, I will convey
to him the *Ziyarah* and salutations of all those who perform his
*Ziyarah* or salute him.” Then Futrus ascended to the heavens.
