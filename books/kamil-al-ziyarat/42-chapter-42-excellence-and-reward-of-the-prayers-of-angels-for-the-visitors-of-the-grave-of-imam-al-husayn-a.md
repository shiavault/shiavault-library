Chapter 42: Excellence And Reward Of The Prayers Of Angels For The Visitors Of The Grave Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A)
===============================================================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Allah has appointed seventy
thousand angels to pray at the grave of Imam Al-Husayn ('a). Each of
their prayers equals a thousand prayers of a human being and the reward
for their prayers is recorded for the Visitors of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) -
may Allah's curse, curse of the angels and curse of all people be on
those who martyred him.

**Tradition 2:** Same as no. 1.
