Chapter 61: Ziyarah Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A) Increases Lifespan And Sustenance; Forsaking It Shortens Life And Decrease Sustenance
================================================================================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam Al-Baqir ('a) said: Order our Shias to visit the
grave of Imam Al-Husayn ('a), because his *Ziyarah* increases
sustenance, prolongs life and keeps afflictions away.

And his *Ziyarah* is obligatory on every believer who believes that
Husayn ('a) is an Imam appointed by Allah.

**Tradition 2:** Imam ('a) said: If a full year passes and one does not
perform the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a), a year from his life will
be reduced by Allah.

If I tell you that some of you die thirty years before your time of
death, I have said the truth and this is because you ignore the
*Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a). So don't omit the *Ziyarah*, for Allah
will prolong your lives and increase sustenance. But if you ignore it,
Allah will shorten your lives and reduce your sustenance. Compete with
one another in his *Ziyarah* and don't forsake it. Indeed, Husayn ('a)
will bear witness on your behalf regarding this to Allah, His Messenger,
Fatimah and Amir Al-Mu’minin ('a).

**Tradition 3:** Same as no. 2.

**Tradition 4:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Perform the *Ziyarah* of Imam
Al-Husayn ('a) even if it is once a year. Anyone, who performs it with
recognition of his rights and without denying his position, will be
rewarded with Paradise, increase in sustenance and immediate happiness,
which will be brought to him by Allah.

**Tradition 5:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Don't omit the *Ziyarah* of
Imam Al-Husayn ('a) and ask your friends to go to his *Ziyarah*, because
Allah will prolong your life, increase sustenance, allow you to live a
life of bliss and die a blissful death, as well as record you among the
successful ones.
