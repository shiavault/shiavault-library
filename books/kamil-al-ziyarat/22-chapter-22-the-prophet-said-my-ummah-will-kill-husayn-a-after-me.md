Chapter 22: The Prophet Said, “My Ummah Will Kill Husayn ('A) After Me.”
========================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam Ja'far As-Sadiq (‘a) said: One day Husayn Ibn Ali
('a) was sitting in the Prophet's lap and the Prophet was playing with
him; making him laugh, when Ayesha said, “Allah's Messenger! How intense
is your attachment to this child!” The Prophet replied, “Woe be to you!
How can I not I love and admire him when he is the fruit of my heart and
the light of my eyes? Indeed, my Ummah will kill him soon. Allah will
give the reward of one of my Hajj for one who goes to his *Ziyarah*
after his martyrdom.” “Allah's Messenger! One of your Hajj?” Ayesha
asked. “Yes, even two of my Hajj,” the Prophet replied. “Allah's
Messenger! Two of your Hajj?” Ayesha asked. “Yes, even four,” the
Prophet replied. Ayesha went on asking and the Prophet went on
increasing until he reached to ninety of his Hajj and Umrahs.

**Tradition 2:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Imam Al-Husayn ('a) was in the
arms of his mother when Allah's Messenger (S) took him and said,
“Allah's curse be upon those who kill you. Allah's curse be upon those
who plunder you. May Allah destroy those who help each another against
you. May Allah judge between me and them.”

Lady Fatimah (‘a) asked, “Father, what are you saying?”

His Eminence, replied: “My daughter! I remembered the calamities that
would befall him after us, and how he will be oppressed, tormented and
deceived. At that time, he will be accompanied by some people who are
like stars, and they will be led to death. As if I can see their camp
and the place where they will be buried.”

Fatimah (‘a) asked, “Father, where is the place that you describe?”

His Eminence replied: It is a place called Karbala. It is the land of
grief and affliction (Karb wa bala) for us and for the Ummah. The most
wicked ones from my Ummah will rise against them. Even if all
inhabitants of heavens and earths intercede for one of those who rise
against Imam Al-Husayn ('a), their intercession will not be accepted,
and they will abide in Hell forever.

Fatimah (‘a) asked, “Father, would this child be killed?”

His Eminence replied: Yes, my daughter. No one before him has been
killed on whom heavens, earths, mountains, angels, beasts and whales in
the ocean cry.

And if these created beings were permitted, no living object would have
survived after the martyrdom of this child. And a group of people who
love us will go to his *Ziyarah*. No one on earth knows Allah better
than them and no one is more earnest to establish our rights than they
are. There is no one on earth other than them who will pay attention to
Imam Al-Husayn ('a). They are lights in the darkness of injustice and
oppression. They are the intercessors of each other, and they will come
to me at my Pool tomorrow. When they come, I will recognize them by
their marks and faces. Followers of every religion will seek their
leaders and they (visitors of Imam Al-Husayn) will look for no one
except us. They are the causes of stability of the earth, and their
blessings shower down on the earth.

**Tradition 3:** Allah's Messenger (S) said: Those who want to live and
die like me and enter Paradise, and hold on to the tree that the
Almighty Allah has Himself planted, should love Ali ('a) and the
successors after him and accept their merits. Because they are the
favourite guides of Allah. He has given them my knowledge and
understanding. And they are my Ahl Al-Bayt ('a) from my flesh and blood.
I will complain to Allah about their enemies from my Ummah, about those
who deny their merits, and about those who cut off relations by
oppressing them. By Allah they will slay my son. May Allah deprive them
of my intercession.

**Tradition 4:** Imam Al-Baqir ('a) said: Allah's Messenger (S) would
hug Imam Al-Husayn ('a) whenever he came to him. Then he would tell Amir
Al-Mu’minin ('a) to take care of him after which he would kiss him and
weep. Imam Al-Husayn ('a) asked, “Father, why are you crying?” “I am
kissing parts of your body which will be struck with swords and
weeping,” the Prophet replied. “Father, will I be killed?” Husayn ('a)
asked. “Yes, by Allah; you, your father and your brother will all be
slain,” the Prophet replied. “Would our tombs be far from each other?”
Imam Al-Husayn ('a) asked. “Yes, my son,” the Prophet replied. “So, who
from your Ummah will come for our *Ziyarah*?” Imam Al-Husayn ('a) asked.
“None but the truthful ones will come to my *Ziyarah*, and the *Ziyarah*
of your father, your brother and you,” the Prophet replied.

**Tradition 5:** Allah's Messenger (S) was seen with Imams Hasan and
Husayn ('a) sitting in his lap. He went on kissing Imam Al-Hasan ('a)
and Imam Al-Husayn ('a) and saying to him, “Woe on those who kill you.”
