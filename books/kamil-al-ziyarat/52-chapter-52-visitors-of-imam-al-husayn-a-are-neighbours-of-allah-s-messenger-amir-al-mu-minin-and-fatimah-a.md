Chapter 52: Visitors Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A) Are Neighbours Of Allah's Messenger, Amir Al-Mu’minin And Fatimah ('A)
==================================================================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: One who want to be in the
neighbourhood of the Prophet, Ali and Fatimah, should not omit the
*Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn (‘a).

**Tradition 2:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) or Imam Al-Baqir ('a) said: One who
wants his destination and abode to be Paradise, should not omit the
*Ziyarah* of the oppressed one (of Karbala).

I asked, “Who is the oppressed one?”

Imam ('a) replied: Imam Al-Husayn ('a), man of Karbala. The Almighty
Allah will make one who goes to his *Ziyarah* longing for him and with
love for Allah's Messenger, love for Fatimah and love for Amir
Al-Mu’minin, sit at the dinner tables of Paradise. He will be eating
with his chiefs while others undergo the process of Judgment.

**Tradition 3:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Allah, the blessed and
exalted, has appointed some angels at the grave of Imam Al-Husayn ('a).
When someone decides to perform the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) and
performs Ghusl, Prophet Muhammad (S) will call out to him:

“O guest of Allah! Glad tidings for you, you will be with me in
Paradise.”
