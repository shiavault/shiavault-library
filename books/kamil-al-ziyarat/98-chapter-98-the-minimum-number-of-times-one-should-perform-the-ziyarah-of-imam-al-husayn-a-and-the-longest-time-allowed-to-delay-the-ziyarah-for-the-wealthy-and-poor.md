Chapter 98: The Minimum Number Of Times One Should Perform The Ziyarah Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A) And The Longest Time Allowed To Delay The Ziyarah For The Wealthy And Poor
========================================================================================================================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: It is an obligation on wealthy
to perform the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) twice a year and on the
poor, once a year.

**Tradition 2:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Perform the *Ziyarah* of Imam
Al-Husayn ('a) once a year.

**Tradition 3:** Same as no. 2.

**Tradition 4:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) was asked about the *Ziyarah* of
Imam Al-Husayn ('a). He ('a) said, “Go once a year, because I don't want
you to be identified.”

**Tradition 5:** Same as no. 1.

**Tradition 6:** Same as no. 4.

**Tradition 7:** Imam Al-Kadhim ('a) said: Don't be negligent of Imam
Al-Husayn ('a). The wealthy should go once every four months and as for
insolvents:

> لَا يُكَلِّفُ اللَّهُ نَفْسًا إِلَّا وُسْعَهَا

***“Allah does not impose on any soul a duty but to the extent of its
ability.” (2:286).***

**Tradition 8:** Same as no. 4.

**Tradition 9:** Same as no. 2.

**Tradition 10:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) was asked, “Is there an obligatory
prayer after the *Ziyarah* of the grave of Imam Al-Husayn ('a)?”

Imam ('a) replied, “There are no obligatory prayers for *Ziyarah*.”

I (the narrator) asked, “How frequently should I go to his *Ziyarah*?”

Imam ('a) replied, “As much as you like.”

**Tradition 11:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said to me (the narrator), “O Ali!
I have heard that some of our Shia allow a year or two to pass without
going to the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a)!”

I replied, “May I be sacrificed on you, I know many who are like this.”

Imam ('a) said, “By Allah, they have turned their backs to their own
fortune, they declined Allah's rewards and they have distanced
themselves from nearness to Muhammad (S).”

I asked, “May I be sacrificed on you, how often should one go to his
*Ziyarah*?”

Imam ('a) replied, “O Ali! If you go to his *Ziyarah* once every month,
do so.”

I said, “I cannot do that, because I am a manual labourer; I have
pending work on my hands and I cannot be absent from my work for even a
day.”

Imam ('a) replied: You are excused, as are other manual labourers. I was
referring to people other than manual labourers; people who can go for
the *Ziyarah* every week. Such people will have no excuse before Allah
and His Messenger on Judgment Day.

I asked, “Is it permissible for them to send someone on their behalf?”

Imam ('a) replied: Yes, but it is better for them and they will have a
greater reward with their Lord if they go themselves, for He will see
them sleepless at night and tired during the day in their journey to
Karbala.

Allah will look at them with a glance, which will inevitably cause them
to dwell with Muhammad and his family in the greatest places of Firdaus.
Hence compete with one another in going to the *Ziyarah* of Imam
Al-Husayn ('a) and be of those who adhere to it.

**Tradition 12:** Same as no. 2.

**Tradition 13:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) was asked, “After one returns from
the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a), how soon should he perform it
again? And how often should one go to his *Ziyarah*? And what is the
longest time one is allowed to stay from his *Ziyarah*?”

Imam ('a) replied: It is not permissible to forsake his *Ziyarah* for
longer than a month (for those who live nearby).

But for those who live far, it should be performed at least once every
three years.

If three years pass and they don't go to his *Ziyarah*, they are
ungrateful to Allah's Messenger and they have dishonoured him, except
those who have a valid excuse.

**Tradition 14:** Ubaidullah Halabi said: I said to Imam As-Sadiq (‘a),
“We perform the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) two or three times a
year.”

Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) replied, “I don't like you to go there frequently. Go
once a year.”

I asked, “How should I invoke blessings on him during the *Ziyarah*?”

Imam ('a) replied, “Stand behind the grave besides his shoulders and
invoke blessings on the Prophet and then invoke blessing on Imam
Al-Husayn ('a).”

**Tradition 15:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: A Muslim should not refrain
from going to the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn's ('a) grave for longer
than four years.

**Tradition 16:** Same point as mentioned before.

**Tradition 17:** Same points as mentioned before.

Then Imam ('a) said: Those who live near his grave, should not go to his
*Ziyarah* less than once a month. But those who live afar, should
perform it at least once every three years.

If three years pass and they don't go to his *Ziyarah*, they are
ungrateful to Allah's Messenger and they have disowned him, except those
who have a valid excuse.

If the visitor of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) knew how happy he made Allah's
Messenger, Amir Al-Mu’minin, Fatimah, the Imams ('a) and the martyrs
among our family and how they pray for him and the rewards prepared for
him in this life and Hereafter, he would have wished that his house was
at the grave of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) as long as he lived.

Indeed, when the visitor leaves Karbala to return after the *Ziyarah*,
his shadow does not cover anything without it praying for him. And when
the sun shines on him, it burns up his sins just like fire burns
firewood.

The sun will not allow any of his sins to remain. Thus, he will return
without any sins while his rank has been elevated for him to a level,
which cannot be achieved even by those who are smeared with their own
blood in the way of Allah.

Then an angel will be appointed on him who will replace him in
performing Imam Al-Husayn's ('a) *Ziyarah* and who will seek forgiveness
for him until he returns or until three years pass or until he dies
before three years.
