Chapter 31: Lamentation Of The Owl On Imam Al-Husayn ('A)
=========================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) asked, “Has anyone of you ever seen
it (the owl) in daytime?”

It was said, “No, it never appears during the day. It only appears at
night.”

Imam ('a) said: The owls previously dwelled in only developed structures
but when Imam Al-Husayn ('a) was martyred, they refused to stay in
developed structures and refused to take anything but ruins as abodes.
Indeed, the owls fast during the day and are aggrieved until nightfall.
At night they lament on Imam Al-Husayn ('a) till the morning.

**Traditions 2, 3 & 4:** Same as no. 1.
