Chapter 107: Excellence Of The Ziyarah Of Abdul Azim Hasani
===========================================================

**Tradition 1:** I went to Imam Hadi ('a) and he asked me, “Where are
you coming from?”

I replied, “I went to the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a).”

Imam ('a) said, “If you had gone to the *Ziyarah* of the grave of Abdil
Azim who is buried near you, you would have been like one has gone to
the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a).
