Chapter 4: Rewards And Excellence Of Praying In The Prophet's Mosque
====================================================================

**Tradition 1:** The narrator asked Imam As-Sadiq (‘a), “Is praying in
the Prophet's mosque same as praying elsewhere in Medina?”

Imam ('a) replied, “No. One ritual prayer in the Prophet's mosque is
equal to a thousand prayers elsewhere; but praying elsewhere in Medina
is just like praying in any other city.”

**Tradition 2:** The narrator asked Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) about praying in
the Prophet's mosque. Imam ('a) replied: Allah's Messenger (S) said: A
prayer in my mosque equals a thousand prayers elsewhere and a prayer in
Masjid Al-Haram equals a thousand prayers in my mosque.

Imam ('a) further said: Indeed, Allah has preferred Mecca to other
cities and has preferred specific areas of Mecca over other areas in it,
saying,

> وَاتَّخِذُوا مِنْ مَقَامِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ مُصَلًّى

***“Take for yourself a place of prayer in the standing-place of
Ibrahim”*** ***(2:125).***

Allah has also preferred some people over others and in His Book,
ordered people to follow and love them.

**Tradition 3:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: A prayer in the Prophet's
mosque equals ten thousand prayers elsewhere.

**Tradition 4:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Pray more in the Prophet's
mosque, for Allah's Messenger (S) has said, “A prayer in my Masjid
equals a thousand prayers in any other, except Masjid Al-Haram, for a
prayer in Masjid Al-Haram equals a thousand prayers in my Masjid.”

**Traditions 5, 6, 7 and 8:** Same as no. 1.
