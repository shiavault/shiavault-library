Chapter 82: Praying In Full At The Grave Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A)
===============================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) was asked, “Should I perform the
*Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a)?”

Imam ('a) replied, “Perform the *Ziyarah* of the Blessed One and pray in
full form near his grave.”

I asked, “Pray in full form besides him?” Imam ('a) replied, “In full
form.”

I said, “But some of our companions narrate that prayers should be
prayed in short?”

Imam ('a) replied, “Those who are (physically) weak can do so.”

**Tradition 2:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: You can pray the obligatory
prayers in full in three locations: Masjid Al-Haram, Masjid of the
Messenger and grave of Imam Al-Husayn ('a).

**Tradition 3:** Same as no. 2.

**Tradition 4:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: The following is among the
rulings in Allah's knowledge: that a traveller can pray in full in four
locations: in Mecca, Medina, Masjid Kufa Masjid and tomb of Imam
Al-Husayn ('a).

**Tradition 5:** Same as no. 4.

**Tradition 6:** Imam Al-Kadhim (‘a) said, “I love for you that which I
love for myself and I hate for you that which I hate for myself. Pray in
full in the two sanctuaries, in Kufa Masjid and at the tomb of Imam
Al-Husayn ('a).”

**Tradition 7,8 & 9:** Same as no. 4.

**Tradition 10:** Imam Al-Kadhim ('a) was asked, “Should pray in short
or in full when I am in Masjid Al-Haram?”

Imam ('a) replied, “You have the choice of praying in short but it is
better you pray in full, because doing more good is always a blessing.”
