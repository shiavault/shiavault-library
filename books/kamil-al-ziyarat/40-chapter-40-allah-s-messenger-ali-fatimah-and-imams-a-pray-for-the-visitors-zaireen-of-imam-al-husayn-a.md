Chapter 40: Allah's Messenger, Ali, Fatimah And Imams ('A) Pray For The Visitors (Zaireen) Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A)
=================================================================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said to me: O Muawiyah! Do not
forsake the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) because of fear, because
one who abandons the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) will regret it so
much that he will wish that his grave was beside him. Do you like Allah
to see you among those for whom Allah's Messenger, Ali, Fatimah and the
Imams ('a) pray?

**Tradition 2:** I asked permission to meet Imam As-Sadiq (‘a). When I
entered, I found him on his prayer mat in his house praying. I waited
until he finished his prayers and then I heard him recite the following
supplication:

O Allah! O one who has chosen us for honour, promised us right of
intercession, chosen us as successors, gave us the knowledge of the past
and that which is to come and made the hearts of people inclined for us!
Forgive me and my brothers who perform the *Ziyarah* of my grandfather,
Imam Al-Husayn ('a) and those who spend their wealth to travel, put
their bodies to trouble seeking our pleasure in hope of the reward You
have kept for associating with us. They perform the *Ziyarah* of Imam
Al-Husayn ('a) to make Your Prophet happy, to obey our commands and to
antagonize our enemies.

By this, they want to please You, so reward them on our behalf by
Rizwan, protect them day and night, compensate their families and
children whom they left behind in the best way and befriend them, keep
them away from the harm of every insolent tyrant and every weak or
strong one from Your creation, keep them away from the evil of every
devil from among men and Jinn, grant them the best of that which they
have requested while they are away from their homes for having preferred
us over their families, children and relatives. O Allah! Our enemies
have criticized them for going to our *Ziyarah* but that did not
restrain them from coming to us and they went against our opponents. So
have mercy on faces changed by the sun in our love. Have mercy on faces
which were kept on the grave of Imam Al-Husayn ('a). Have mercy on those
eyes which shed tears on us with sympathy. Have mercy on those hearts
which have become restless and distressed for us and have mercy on their
lamentations for us. O Allah! I entrust these bodies and souls to you
till the day of great thirst when you will quench their thirst with Hauz
Kauthar.

Muawiyah Ibn Wahab continued: Imam ('a) continued this supplication in
prostration. When he finished, I said:

“May I be sacrificed on you! I think that if this supplication is in
favour of even those who don't know Allah, the Mighty and Sublime, I
think that Hellfire will not touch them!

By Allah, I wish I had gone to the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a),
instead of going to Hajj.”

Imam ('a) said, “You live so close. What prevents you from his
*Ziyarah*? O Muawiyah! Don't forsake it?”

I replied, “May I be sacrificed on you! I did not think that it was so
important and it had such great rewards.”

Imam ('a) said, “Muawiyah! Those who pray for the visitors of Imam
Al-Husayn ('a) in the heavens are more than those who pray for them on
earth.”

**Tradition 3:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Muawiyah! Don't forsake the
*Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) because of fear. Because he that
abandons the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) will regret it so much
that he will wish his grave was beside him. Do you like Allah to see you
among those for whom Allah's Messenger, Ali, Fatimah the Imams ('a)
pray?

Do you like to be of those who return forgiven of their past sins and to
return from his *Ziyarah* with seventy years of sins forgiven? Do you
like to be of those who will not have any sins about which they will be
questioned when they leave this life? Do you like to be of those whose
hands Allah's Messenger (S) will shake tomorrow?

**Tradition 4:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Indeed, Fatimah (‘a), daughter
of Muhammad (S), visits one who performs the *Ziyarah* of the grave of
her son, Imam Al-Husayn ('a) and seeks forgiveness for his sins.
