Chapter 54: Reward Of One Who Performs The Ziyarah Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A) With Recognition Of His Rights
========================================================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam Al-Kadhim ('a) said: Allah will forgive all past
and future sins of one who performs the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a)
with recognition of his rights.

**Tradition 2:** The narrator says: I said to Imam Ja'far As-Sadiq (‘a):
People narrate that those who go to the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a)
will get the reward of a Hajj and an Umrah. The Imam said: By Allah, all
the past and future sins will be forgiven of those who go for his
*Ziyarah*, knowing his rights.

**Tradition 3:** Imam Al-Kadhim ('a) said: The least reward for one who
performs the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) by the banks of Euphrates,
with recognition of his rights and believing in his sanctity and
Wilayat, is that all of his past and future sins will be forgiven.

**Traditions 4-13:** Same points mentioned in previous traditions.

**Tradition 14:** I (the narrator) said to Imam Al-Kadhim ('a): May I be
sacrificed on you! Many go to the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) from
those who believe in imamate and those who deny it and even ladies
travel to him. It has become so well known that I stopped going for fear
of being identified.

The Imam ('a) did not respond and remained silent for some time.

Then he ('a) looked at me and said, “O Iraqi! Even if others go openly,
you should also go but don't identify yourself. By Allah, no one goes to
the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) with recognition of his rights
without Allah forgiving all his past and future sins.”

**Tradition 15:** Same as no. 2.

**Tradition 16:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: One who performs the
*Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) with recognition of his rights is like
one who has performed Hajj three times in the company of Allah's
Messenger.

**Tradition 17:** Imam Ar-Ridha’ ('a) said: One who performs the
*Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) with recognition of his rights will
speak to Allah atop His throne (on Judgment Day). “Surely those who
guard (against evil) shall be in gardens and rivers in the seat of
honour with a most Powerful King.”
