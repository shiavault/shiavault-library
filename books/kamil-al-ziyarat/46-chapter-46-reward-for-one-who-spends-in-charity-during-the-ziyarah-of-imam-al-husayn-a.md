Chapter 46: Reward For One Who Spends In Charity During The Ziyarah Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A)
==========================================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: One who performs the *Ziyarah*
of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) has connected with Allah's Messenger and with us.
It is unlawful to slander him and it is unlawful for Hell to touch his
flesh. Allah will compensate him for every Dirham he spends with ten
thousand cities, which will be written for him in a scroll. Allah will
look after all of his needs and everything that he leaves behind will be
protected. He will not ask Allah for anything without Him granting it
either immediately or in future.

**Traditions 2, 3, 4, 5:** Same points as mentioned in traditions quoted
above.
