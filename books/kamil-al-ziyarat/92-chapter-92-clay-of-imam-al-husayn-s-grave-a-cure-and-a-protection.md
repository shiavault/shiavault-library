Chapter 92: Clay Of Imam Al-Husayn's Grave: A Cure And A Protection
===================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam Ar-Ridha’ ('a) sent me a bundle of clothes, in the
middle of which was some clay, I asked the person who brought them about
this clay.

He said, “It is the clay of Imam Al-Husayn's ('a) grave. Imam Ar-Ridha’
('a) never sends clothes or anything else to anyone without putting some
of this clay within it and he says, 'It is protection with the
permission of Allah.”

**Tradition 2:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Make the first thing which is
rubbed on the palate of your newborn children Imam Al-Husayn's ('a)
clay, for indeed, it is protection.

**Tradition 3:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) was asked, “Can I take some clay of
Imam Al-Husayn's ('a) grave and keep it with me to seek blessings from
it?”

Imam ('a) replied, “No problem.”

**Tradition 4:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: The clay of the tomb, where
Imam Al-Husayn ('a) is buried, is a cure for every disease and a
protection against any fear.

**Tradition 5:** Same as no. 4.

**Tradition 6:** Same points as mentioned in previous traditions.
