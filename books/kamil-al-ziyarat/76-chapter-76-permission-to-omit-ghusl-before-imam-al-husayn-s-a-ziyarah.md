Chapter 76: Permission To Omit Ghusl Before Imam Al-Husayn's ('A) Ziyarah
=========================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) was asked, “ls Ghusl obligatory
before the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a)?”

Imam ('a) replied, “No.”

**Tradition 2 & 3:** Same as no. 1.

**Tradition 4:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: If you are near him and it is
possible to do ghusl, do so; otherwise perform wudhu and then do his
*Ziyarah*.

**Tradition 5:** Same as no. 1.

**Tradition 6:** I (the narrator) said to Imam As-Sadiq (‘a), “Sometimes
when we perform the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) it is difficult to
make ghusl, because of cold or for some other reason.”

Imam ('a) replied, “So many merits will be recorded for one who goes to
Euphrates and performs ghusl before *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a),
that they cannot be counted. And if he returns anytime (in future) to
the place where he performed ghusl and instead performs wudhu before the
*Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a), the same reward will be recorded for
him.”
