Chapter 37: Imam Al-Husayn ('A) Is The Chief Of Martyrs
=======================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Perform the *Ziyarah* of Imam
Al-Husayn ('a) and do not ignore him. Because he is the leader of youths
of Paradise and the chief of martyrs.

**Tradition 2:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) was asked in Medina, “Where are the
graves of the martyrs?”

Imam ('a) replied: Is not the best martyr buried near you? I swear by
One who has my life in His Hand, four thousand angels are present around
the grave of Imam Al-Husayn ('a), dishevelled, covered with dust and
crying over him till Judgment Day.

**Tradition 3:** Same as no. 4.

**Tradition 4:** I (the narrator) went to Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) while I was
with him, my bondmaid arrived and said, “I brought the horse for you.”

Imam ('a) asked, “Umm Saeed, what is the horse for and where do you
intend to go?”

I replied, “To go for the *Ziyarah* of the martyrs.

Imam ('a) said, “Postpone it for today. How strange you people of Iraq
are? You perform the *Ziyarah* of martyrs of Uhud from a long distance
but forsake the *Ziyarah* of the Chief of martyrs!”

I asked, “Who is the Chief of martyrs?”

Imam ('a) said, “Husayn Ibn Ali ('a)”.

I said, “I can't go for his *Ziyarah*, because I am a lady.”

Imam ('a) replied, “There is nothing wrong in ladies going for his
*Ziyarah*.”

I asked, 'What is the reward for it?”

Imam ('a) replied, “Reward of a Hajj and an Umrah, I’tikaf for two
months in Masjid Al-Haram while fasting and this and this.” Umm Saeed
says that the Imam extended and closed his arms thrice.

**Tradition 5:** Same as no. 4.

**Tradition 6:** Almost same as no. 4.

**Tradition 7:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: There is no martyr but that he
wishes to be with Imam Al-Husayn ('a), so that he can enter Paradise
with him.
