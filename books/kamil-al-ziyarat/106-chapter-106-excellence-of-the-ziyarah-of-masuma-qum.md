Chapter 106: Excellence Of The Ziyarah Of Masuma Qum
====================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam Ar-Ridha’ ('a) was asked about the *Ziyarah* of
Fatimah, daughter of Imam Al-Kadhim ('a).

Imam ('a) said, “One who goes to her *Ziyarah* will be rewarded with
Paradise.”

**Tradition 2:** Same as no. 1.
