Chapter 43: Ziyarah Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A) Is Obligatory On All
===============================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Order our Shia to perform the
*Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a), because it is obligatory on every
believer who testifies that Husayn ('a) is an Imam appointed by Allah,
the Mighty and Sublime.

**Tradition 2:** Imam Ar-Ridha’ ('a) said: There is a binding contract
on every Shia and follower towards him Imam and the most perfect and
beautiful way to fulfil this contract is to perform the *Ziyarah* of the
grave of that Imam.

On Judgment Day, Imam ('a) will intercede for one who performs his
*Ziyarah* with longing and believe in that which he has been ordered to
believe.

**Tradition 3:** Same as no. 1.

**Tradition 4:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Even if you go to Hajj every
year but don't perform the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a), you have
abandoned one of your obligations towards Allah and His messenger,
because the right of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) is made obligatory on every
Muslim by the Almighty Allah.
