Chapter 56: Reward For One Who Performs The Ziyarah Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A) Longing For Him
==========================================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Allah will write one who
performs the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) longing for him as one who
will be safe on Judgment Day. He will give the scroll of deeds in his
right hand. He will be under the standard of Husayn (‘a) until he enters
Paradise and Imam Al-Husayn ('a) will make him reside at his stage in
Paradise. Indeed, Allah is Mighty and Wise.

**Tradition 2:** Same as Chapter 52, Tradition no. 2.

**Tradition 3:** Imam Al-Baqir (‘a) said: “If people knew the reward for
performing the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a), their souls would leave
their bodies in remorse, and they would die longing for it.”

I asked, “What ls the reward for going to his *Ziyarah*?”

Imam ('a) replied: Allah will record the reward of a thousand accepted
Hajj, a thousand. accepted Umrahs, a thousand martyrs of Badr, fasting
of a thousand people, a thousand accepted prostrations and the reward of
freeing a thousand slaves in the way of Allah for one who goes to the
*Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) longing for him. He will be protected
for a whole year from every calamity, the least of which is Shaitan. And
Allah will appoint a noble angel to protect him from front, behind,
right, left, above and below.

If' he dies that year, angels of divine mercy will be present with him,
bathe and shroud him and seek forgiveness for him. They will also follow
him till the grave and widen it as far as the eye can see and Allah will
keep him safe from the squeeze of the grave and from fear of angels,
Munkar and Nakir. A door will also be opened from his gave to Paradise.
On Judgment Day, scroll of deeds will be given in his right hand and he
will shine with a light, which illuminates from the east to west. And an
announcer will call out. He is one who performed the *Ziyarah* of Imam
Al-Husayn (‘a) with enthusiasm. After that no one will remain on
Judgment Day who will not wish that he had also performed the *Ziyarah*
of Imam Al-Husayn ('a).

**Tradition 4:** Same points as mentioned before.

**Tradition 5:** I (the narrator) complained to Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) about
the way in which I suffer when I tell my relatives and children about
the reward of the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a). I complained that
they call me a liar, accusing me of forging lies against Imam Ja'far
As-Sadiq (‘a).

Imam ('a) replied: O Dharih! Let the people go wherever they want! 'By
Allah, Allah boasts about one who performs the *Ziyarah* of Imam
Al-Husayn (‘a) to the proximate angels and the carriers of His throne
who welcome those who arrive at the grave of Imam Al-Husayn (‘a).

He says: Don't you see the visitor of the grave of Imam Al-Husayn ('a)
who came to him longing for him and for Fatimah, daughter of Allah's
Messenger? I swear by My glory, magnificence and greatness, I will
ordain that they be honoured. I will take them to My Paradise; the
Paradise, which I have prepared for My friends, prophets and messengers.

O My angels! These are visitors of Husayn, beloved of Muhammad, My
messenger, and Muhammad is My beloved. One who loves Me, loves the one I
love and one who loves one I love, loves the one loved by him \[Husayn
('a)\]. One who hates My beloved, hates Me. It is My right to punish
those who hate Me with My most intense chastisement, to burn them with
blazing flames of My fire and to make Hell their abode, where I will
punish them with such intense chastisement with which I will not punish
anyone else.
