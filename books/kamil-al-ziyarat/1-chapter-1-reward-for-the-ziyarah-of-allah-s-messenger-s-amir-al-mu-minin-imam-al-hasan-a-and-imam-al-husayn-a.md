Chapter 1: Reward For The Ziyarah Of Allah's Messenger (S), Amir Al-Mu’minin, Imam Al-Hasan ('A) And Imam Al-Husayn ('A)
========================================================================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said:

> بَيْنَمَا الْحُسَيْنُ بْنُ عَلِيٍّ ع فِي حَجْرِ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ ص إِذْ رَفَعَ رَأْسَهُ فَقَالَ لَهُ يَا أَبَتِ مَا
> لِمَنْ زَارَكَ بَعْدَ مَوْتِكَ فَقَالَ يَا بُنَيَّ مَنْ أَتَانِي زَائِراً بَعْدَ مَوْتِي فَلَهُ الْجَنَّةُ وَ مَنْ
> أَتَى أَبَاكَ زَائِراً بَعْدَ مَوْتِهِ فَلَهُ الْجَنَّةُ وَ مَنْ أَتَى أَخَاكَ زَائِراً بَعْدَ مَوْتِهِ فَلَهُ
> الْجَنَّةُ وَ مَنْ أَتَاكَ زَائِراً بَعْدَ مَوْتِكَ فَلَهُ الْجَنَّةُ.

One day while Imam Al-Husayn ('a) was sitting on the lap of Allah's
Messenger (S), he looked up and asked, “Father, what is the reward for
one who performs your *Ziyarah* after your passing away?”

Allah's Messenger (S) replied: “My son, one who performs my *Ziyarah*
after my passing away will be rewarded with Paradise; one who performs
the *Ziyarah* of your father after his passing away will be rewarded
with Paradise; one who performs the *Ziyarah* of your brother after his
passing away will be rewarded with Paradise; and one who performs your
*Ziyarah* after your passing away will be rewarded with Paradise.”

**Tradition 2:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said:

> قَالَ الْحُسَيْنُ ص لِرَسُولِ اللَّهِ ص مَا جَزَاءُ مَنْ زَارَكَ- فَقَالَ يَا بُنَيَّ مَنْ زَارَنِي حَيّاً أَوْ
> مَيِّتاً أَوْ زَارَ أَبَاكَ أَوْ زَارَ أَخَاكَ أَوْ زَارَكَ كَانَ حَقّاً عَلَيَّ أَنْ أَزُورَهُ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ
> حَتَّى أُخَلِّصَهُ مِنْ ذُنُوبِهِ.

Imam Al-Husayn ('a) asked Allah's Messenger (S), “What is the reward for
one who performs your *Ziyarah*?”

The Messenger replied: “My son, on Judgment Day, it will be incumbent on
me to visit one who visits me during my lifetime or after my passing
away, or one who has performed *Ziyarah* of your father, or your
brother, or your *Ziyarah*; and to save him from the chastisement of his
sins.”

**Tradition 3:** Allah's Messenger (S) said:

> قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ ص‌ يَا عَلِيُّ مَنْ زَارَنِي فِي حَيَاتِي أَوْ بَعْدَ مَوْتِي أَوْ زَارَكَ فِي حَيَاتِكَ
> أَوْ بَعْدَ مَوْتِكَ أَوْ زَارَ ابْنَيْكَ فِي حَيَاتِهِمَا أَوْ بَعْدَ مَوْتِهِمَا ضَمِنْتُ لَهُ يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ
> أَنْ أُخَلِّصَهُ مِنْ أَهْوَالِهَا وَ شَدَائِدِهَا حَتَّى أُصَيِّرَهُ مَعِي فِي دَرَجَتِي.

O Ali! I guarantee to one who performs my *Ziyarah* during my lifetime
or after my passing away, or performs your *Ziyarah* during your
lifetime or after your passing away, or performs the *Ziyarah* of your
two sons during their lives or after their passing away; that I will
save him from terrors and difficulties of Judgment Day and take him with
me to my level.

**Tradition 4:** Allah's Messenger (S) said: On Judgment Day, I will
visit one who has performed my *Ziyarah* or the *Ziyarah* of anyone from
my progeny; till I save him from the terrors of that day.

**Tradition 5:** Same as no. 2.
