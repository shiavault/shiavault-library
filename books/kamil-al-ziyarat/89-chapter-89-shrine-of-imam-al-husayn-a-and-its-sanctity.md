Chapter 89: Shrine Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A) And Its Sanctity
==========================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: The grave of Imam Al-Husayn
('a) is a garden from the gardens of Paradise since the day he was
buried in it.

**Tradition 2:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: The sacred boundaries of the
grave of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) are one Farsakh by one Farsakh from each of
its four sides.

**Tradition 3:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: The sacred boundary of the
grave of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) is five Farsakh from each of the four sides
of the grave.

**Tradition 4:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: There is a known boundary for
the location of the grave of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) and whoever knows it
and seeks refuge in it, will be protected.

I said, “May I be sacrificed on you, describe its boundary for me.”

Imam ('a) replied, “According to the current location, measures
twenty-five cubits from the direction of his feet, twenty-five cubits
from his face, twenty-five cubits from his back and twenty-five cubits
from his back and twenty-five cubits from his head.

Imam ('a) added: The location of the grave of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) has
been a garden from the gardens of Paradise since the day he was buried
and the deeds of his visitor ascend to the heavens from this site.

There is not any prophet or angel in the heavens who does not ask Allah
for permission to perform the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a). So,
there is always a crowd descending and a crowd ascending.

Imam Al-Husayn ('a) are twenty cubits by twenty cubits and this site is
a garden from Paradise...
