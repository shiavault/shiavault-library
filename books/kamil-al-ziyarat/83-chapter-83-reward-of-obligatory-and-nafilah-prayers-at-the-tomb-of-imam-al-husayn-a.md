Chapter 83: Reward Of Obligatory And Nafilah Prayers At The Tomb Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A)
=======================================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam Al-Baqir ('a) said to a man: O so and so, what
prevents you at times of need from going to the tomb of Imam Al-Husayn
('a), praying four Rakats and then asking Allah for you needs? Indeed,
praying an obligatory prayer near his grave equals a Hajj and praying a
Nafilah prayer, equals an Umrah.

**Tradition 2:** Same point as mentioned in Chapter 79, Tradition 5.

**Tradition 3:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) was asked, 'What is the reward for
one who performs the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a)?”

Imam ('a) replied, “Allah will record the reward of performing a Hajj
and an Umrah for one who visits the grave of Imam Al-Husayn ('a),
performs his *Ziyarah* and prays two or four Rakats prayer besides him.”

I asked, “May I be sacrificed on you, is this reward same for one who
performs the *Ziyarah* of the grave of other Imams whose obedience is
obligatory?”

Imam ('a) replied, “The reward is the same for anyone who goes to the
*Ziyarah* of the grave of the other Imams whose obedience is
obligatory.”

**Tradition 4:** Same points as mentioned before.
