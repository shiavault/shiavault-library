Chapter 74: Reward Of The Ziyarah Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A) On Any Day, Other Than Eid And Arafah Day
==================================================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Allah will record twenty
accepted Hajj and umrahs and reward of fighting on the side of a divine
messenger or a just Imam for any believer who goes to the *Ziyarah* of
Imam Al-Husayn (‘a) with recognition of his rights on any other day than
an Eid and Arafah Day.

**Tradition 2:** Same as Chapter 49, Tradition no. 5.

**Tradition 3:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Allah will forgive one who
performs the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) every Friday: He will not
leave the world with any sorrows about it and will dwell with Imam
Al-Husayn ('a) in Paradise.

Then he said: O Dawood! Who does not like to be the neighbour of Imam
Al-Husayn ('a) in Paradise?

I replied, “One who has not achieved salvation.”

**Tradition 4:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) was asked, “What is the reward for
one who performs the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) every month?”

Imam (‘a) replied, “He will earn the reward of a hundred thousand
martyrs like the Badr martyrs.

**Tradition 5:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: On the night of Qadr,

> فِيهَا يُفْرَقُ كُلُّ أَمْرٍ حَكِيمٍ

***“Therein every wise affair is made distinct” (44:4).***

an announcer will announce from inside the Arsh, “Indeed, Allah has
forgiven everyone who came to the *Ziyarah* of the grave of Imam
Al-Husayn ('a) on this night.”

**Tradition 6:** Same points as mentioned before.
