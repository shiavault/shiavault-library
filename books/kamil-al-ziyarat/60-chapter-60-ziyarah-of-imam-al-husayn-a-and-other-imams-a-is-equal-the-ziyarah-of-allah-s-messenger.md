Chapter 60: Ziyarah Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A) And Other Imams ('A) Is Equal The Ziyarah Of Allah's Messenger
=========================================================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: One who wants to be able to
look at Allah on Judgment Day and reduce his troubles and fear for that
which is coming, should perform the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a)
more, because the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) is the *Ziyarah* of
Allah's' Messenger (S).

**Traditions 2 & 3:** Same as no. 1.

**Tradition 4:** Same points as mentioned before.
