Chapter 2: Reward For The Ziyarah Of Allah's Messenger (S)
==========================================================

**Tradition 1:** Allah's Messenger (S) said: On Judgment Day, I will
intercede for one who performs my *Ziyarah*.

**Tradition 2:** When Imam Jawad ('a) was asked about the reward for one
who performs the *Ziyarah* of Allah's Messenger (S) solely for (the
purpose of) *Ziyarah*, Imam ('a) replied, “Paradise.”

**Tradition 3 & 4:** Same as no. 2.

**Tradition 5:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) ordered me (the narrator) to pray in
the Prophet's mosque as much as I could. He told me that I would not be
able to pray there whenever I wanted to; so, I should make most of the
opportunity.

Then Imam ('a) asked .me, “Do you visit the grave of Allah's Messenger
(S)? “Yes,” I replied. Imam ('a) said, “Indeed, Allah's Messenger (S)
hears you if you perform his *Ziyarah* from near and he shall be
informed if you perform his *Ziyarah* from far.”

**Tradition 6:** Almost same as no. 5.

**Tradition 7 & 8:** Same as no. 2.

**Tradition 9:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Allah's Messenger (S) said: On
Judgment Day, I will turn away from one who goes to Mecca for Hajj but
does not come to Medina for my *Ziyarah*. It is necessary for one who
performs my *Ziyarah* to be included in my intercession and Paradise is
guaranteed for one who is interceded by me. Then he said: One who dies
in one of the two sanctuaries (Mecca or Medina) will not be brought to
judgment. He will be counted as one who died while migrating to Allah
and will be resurrected with fighters of Badr on Judgment Day.

**Tradition 1O:** Allah's Messenger (S) said: On Judgment Day, I will
intercede for one who comes to my *Ziyarah*.

**Tradition 11:** Allah's Messenger (S) said: One who performs my
*Ziyarah* during my lifetime or after my passing away will be in my
neighbourhood on Judgment Day.

**Tradition 12:** Allah's Messenger (S) said: One who performs my
*Ziyarah* after my passing away is like one who visited me during my
lifetime, and I will be his witness and intercessor on Judgment Day.

**Tradition 13:** Same as no. 12

**Tradition 14:** Allah's Messenger (S) said: On Judgment Day, I will
intercede for one who comes to Medina for my *Ziyarah*, expecting divine
rewards.

**Tradition 15:** Imam Jawad ('a) was asked, “What is the reward for one
who performs the *Ziyarah* of Allah's Messenger (S) with sincerity?”

Imam ('a) replied, “Allah will admit him to Paradise.”

**Tradition 16:** Same as no. 12

**Tradition 17:** Allah's Messenger (S) said: One who comes to the
*Ziyarah* of my grave after my passing away is like one who has migrated
to me during my lifetime. So, if you cannot come for my *Ziyarah*,
convey your salutation to me, for it is sure to reach me.

**Tradition 18:** Same as Chapter 1, Tradition 2.

**Tradition 19:** Same as no. 18

**Tradition 20:** Imam Al-Baqir ('a) said: Indeed, *Ziyarah* of the
grave of Allah's Messenger (S) is equal to an accepted Hajj performed in
the company of Allah's Messenger (S).

**Tradition 21:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) was asked, “What is the reward of
one who performs the *Ziyarah* of the grave of Allah's Messenger (S)?”

Imam ('a) replied, “He is like one who performs the *Ziyarah* of Allah
on His Throne.”
