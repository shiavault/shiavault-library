Chapter 47: That Which Is Detestable To Take To The Ziyarah Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A)
==================================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: I have come to know that some
people go to the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn carrying different kinds of
foods including sweet meats and other deserts. Indeed, if they had gone
to visit the graves of their loved ones, they would not have done thus.

**Tradition 2:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) asked, “Do you perform the *Ziyarah*
of the grave of Imam Al-Husayn ('a)?”

The narrator replied, “Yes.”

Imam ('a) asked, “Do you carry different foods with you?” ·.

I replied, “Yes.”

Imam ('a) said, “It is sure that if you visit the graves of your own
parents, you don't do this.” ·

I asked, “Then what should we eat?” Imam (‘a) replied, “Bread and milk”

**Tradition 3:** Same as no. 1.

**Tradition 4:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said, “Going to the *Ziyarah* of
Imam Al-Husayn ('a) is better than not going. But sometimes not going is
better than going.”

I said, “You have broken my back with this statement.”

Imam ('a) said: By Allah, when one of you go to the graves of your
fathers you are distressed and aggrieved. But when you go to Imam
Al-Husayn ('a) you carry different foods. It is better not to go, unless
you go aggrieved and dejected.
