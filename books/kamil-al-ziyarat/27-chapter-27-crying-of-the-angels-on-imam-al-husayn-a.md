Chapter 27: Crying Of The Angels On Imam Al-Husayn ('A)
=======================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) asked: What's wrong with you? Why
don't you go to him (to the grave of Imam Al-Husayn)? Because four
thousand angels are present at his grave and they cry on him until
Judgment Day.

**Tradition 2:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Four thousand angels came down
to fight for Imam Al-Husayn ('a) but he did not permit them. When they
came down again to seek permission, Imam Al-Husayn ('a) had already been
martyred. So, they remain at his grave, dishevelled and covered with
dust, crying over him until Judgment Day. Their leader is an angel
called Mansur.

**Tradition 3:** Same as no. 1.

**Tradition 4:** Same as no. 2.

**Tradition 5:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Since the day Imam Al-Husayn
('a) was martyred, Allah has appointed seventy thousand angels who are
dishevelled and covered with dust; to invoke blessings on Imam Al-Husayn
('a) and pray for mercy on him as long as Allah wills, that is till the
time of the Qa’im ('a).

**Traditions 6, 7, 8 and 9:** Same as no. 4.

**Tradition 10:** Imam Al-Baqir ('a) said: Four thousand dishevelled
angels, covered with dust cry over Imam Al-Husayn ('a) until Judgment
Day. No one who comes to his *Ziyarah* but that they welcome him. No
visitor of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) becomes ill, but that they visit them and
none of them dies, but that they are present near him.

**Tradition 11:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Allah has appointed four
thousand dishevelled angles, covered with dust, at the grave of Imam
Al-Husayn ('a) to cry on him from sunrise to midday. At midday they are
replaced with another four thousand who cry over him till sunrise.

**Tradition 12:** Same as no.4.

**Tradition 13:** Same as no. 4 and 10.

**Tradition 14:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Allah has appointed seventy
thousand angels to the grave of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) who are dishevelled
and covered with dust to cry over him until Judgment Day. They pray at
his grave and each of their prayers equals a thousand prayers of human
beings and the rewards of their prayers is for one who performs the
*Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a).

**Tradition 15:** Same as no. 2.

**Tradition 16:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: When you go for the *Ziyarah*
of Imam Al-Husayn ('a), maintain silence and except for good do not say
anything, because angels of the night and day who are the guards and
keepers, go to the angels who are in the holy shrine and shake hands
with them. But the angels of the holy shrine do not speak to them,
because they are crying so intensely. So, they wait from them until noon
or sunrise and then speak to them about some affairs of the heavens. The
angels of the holy shrine abstain from speaking between these sunrise
and noon and their crying and supplicating does not subside. Indeed, the
Keepers do not distract the angels of the holy shrine between these two
times. But the angels of the holy shrine become distracted when you ask
(the angels of the holy shrine) or is it the angels of the holy shrine
who ask (the Keepers)?”

I asked, “May I be sacrificed on you? What do the angels ask each other
and which group asks the other? Is it the keepers who ask (the angels of
the holy shrine) or is it the angels of the holy shrine who ask (the
Keepers)?”

Imam ('a) replied. “The angels of the holy shrine ask the Keepers
because the angels of the holy shrine do not move from their place. But
the Keepers descend from the heavens and ascend back to them.”

I asked, “What do the angels of the holy shrine ask the keepers?”

Imam ('a) replied: The Keepers pass by Ismail, the Angel of Air, in
their ascent (to the heavens) and sometimes they reach him at the same
time when the Prophet, Fatimah, Hasan, Husayn and the (other) Imams
('a), who have passed away, are there (with Ismail). They (Ahl Al-Bayt)
ask the Keepers about some matters and about those of you (visitors) who
are present in the holy shrine.

They (Ahl Al-Bayt) say to the Keepers, “Give them (those who have gone
to the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn) glad tidings with your prayers.”

The Keepers ask, “How we can give them glad tidings when they cannot
hear us?”

Ahl Al-Bayt ('a) will reply, “Congratulate them and pray for them on our
behalf. Indeed, these are our glad tidings to them. When they want to
leave, surround them and rub your wings on them, so that they can feel
your presence. Indeed, we entrust them to He whose trust will never be
lost.”

Imam ('a) continued: If the visitor of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) and the
people knew the blessings of the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a), they
would have fought one another with swords and they would have sold their
belongings to go to his *Ziyarah*.

When Lady Fatimah (‘a) who is accompanied by one thousand prophets, a
thousand truthfuls, a thousand martyrs and a million Karubiyyin who all
support her in crying \[over Husayn ('a)\] looks at those who perform
the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a), she lets out a whoop in such a way
that no angel remains in the heavens who does not cry, sympathizing with
her cry. But Lady Fatimah (‘a) is not pacified until the Prophet (S)
goes to her and says:

“My daughter! You have made the inhabitants of the heavens cry and you
have distracted them from glorifying and sanctifying (Allah). So, stop
crying that they can glorify (Allah).

> إِنَّ اللَّهَ بَالِغُ أَمْرِهِ

***“Indeed, Allah accomplishes His purposes” (65:3).***

Lady Fatimah (‘a) looks at those of you who are present at the grave of
Imam Al-Husayn ('a) and she asks Allah to shower you with all of the
blessings. Imam ('a) continued, “Do not abstain from the *Ziyarah* of
Imam Al-Husayn ('a). Indeed, the blessings of going to his *Ziyarah* are
much greater than that which can be counted.”

**Tradition 17:** The narrator said to Imam As-Sadiq (‘a), “May I be
sacrificed on you! How short are your lives, O Ahl Al-Bayt! And how
close are your deaths to one another while the entire creation needs
you?”

Imam ('a) replied: There is a scroll for each of us which contains
everything that we need to do during our time. Once every order on that
scroll has been carried out, we know that our death is imminent. Allah's
Messenger (S) comes to us and informs us that our death has come and he
informs us of the rewards Allah has prepared for us. Indeed, Imam
Al-Husayn ('a) read the sheet that was entrusted to him and that which
would happen and that which was yet to come were explained to him. There
were a few things left on Husayn's sheet, which had not been fulfilled
when he rose to fight. Those matters were that the angels asked Allah
for permission to help Imam Al-Husayn ('a) and Allah granted it to them.
While the angels descended to the earth, they found that Imam
Al-Husayn's time had passed and he had been martyred. Hence, they said
(to Allah):

“O Lord! You gave us permission to descend to earth to support Husayn
but when we arrived, you had already captured his soul?”

The Almighty Allah revealed to them, 'Stay besides his grave until you
see him rise again and then support him. Cry over him and over losing
the opportunity to support him.

Indeed, you have been chosen to support him and to cry over him.’ The
angels were aggrieved, and they cried and lamented over losing the
opportunity to support Husayn ('a). And when he rises, they will be
among his supporters.
