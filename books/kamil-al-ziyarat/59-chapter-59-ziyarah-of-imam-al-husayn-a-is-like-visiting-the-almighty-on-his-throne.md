Chapter 59: Ziyarah Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A) Is Like Visiting The Almighty On His Throne
======================================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) was asked, 'What is the reward of
one who performs the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a)?” Imam ('a)
replied, “He is like one who has performed the *Ziyarah* of Allah on His
Throne.” I asked, “What is the reward of one who performs the *Ziyarah*
of anyone of you members of Ahl Al-Bayt ('a)?” Imam ('a) replied, “He is
like one who have performed the *Ziyarah* of Allah's Messenger (S).”

**Tradition 2:** Same as no. 1.

**Tradition 3:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Allah will include one who
performs the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) with recognition of his
rights, as one in Alaa Illiyyin (the highest position in Paradise).

**Traditions 4, 5 & 6:** Same as no. 3.

**Tradition 7:** Imam Ar-Ridha’ ('a) said: One who performs the
*Ziyarah* of the grave of my father in Baghdad is like one who has
performed the *Ziyarah* of Allah's Messenger and Amir Al-Mu’minin (‘a),
except that Allah's Messenger and Amir Al-Mu’minin (‘a) have their own
respective status.

Imam (‘a) further said: One who performs the *Ziyarah* of the grave of
Imam Al-Husayn ('a) by the banks of Euphrates is like one who has
visited Allah on His Throne.

**Traditions 8, 9 & 10:** Same as no. 3.

**Tradition 11:** I (the narrator) performed Hajj every year but one
year I did not go. The following year, after Hajj I met Imam As-Sadiq
(‘a), who asked me, “O Bashir! What prevented you from Hajj last year?''

I replied, “May I be sacrificed on you! Some people owed me money and I
was worried that I might lose it. So, I spent Arafah Day at the grave of
Imam Al-Husayn ('a).

Imam (‘a) said, “Then you did not miss anything compared to those who
were present at Arafat. Bashir, one who performs the *Ziyarah* of Imam
Al-Husayn (‘a) with recognition of his rights, is like one who visits
Allah on His Throne:”

**Tradition 12:** Same as no. 3.
