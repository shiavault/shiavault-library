Chapter 51: Days Spent In Ziyarah Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A) Are Not Counted In One's Lifespan
==========================================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Days spent in *Ziyarah* of
Imam Al-Husayn ('a) are not counted as part of one’s lifespan; and are
not reduced from the time of one’s death.
