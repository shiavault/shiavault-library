Chapter 99: Reward For The Ziyarah Of Imam Al-Kadhim And Imam Jawad ('A)
========================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam Ar-Ridha’ ('a) was asked if the *Ziyarah* of Imam
Al-Kadhim ('a) is same as the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a).

Imam ('a) replied, “Yes.”

**Tradition 2:** Imam Ar-Ridha’ ('a) was asked, “Should I perform the
*Ziyarah* of the grave of Imam Al-Kadhim ('a) in Baghdad?”

Imam ('a) replied, “If you must, then perform the *Ziyarah* from behind
the covers.”

**Tradition 3:** Same as no. 1.

**Tradition 4:** Imam Ar-Ridha’ ('a) was asked about the *Ziyarah* of
Imam Al-Kadhim ('a). He ('a) replied, “Pray in the Masjids around the
grave.”

**Tradition 5:** Imam Ar-Ridha’ ('a) was asked, “What are the rewards
for the *Ziyarah* of the grave of your father, Allah's blessings be on
him?”

Imam ('a) replied, “Go to his *Ziyarah*!”

I (the narrator) asked, “What are its merits?''

Imam ('a) replied, “Same as the merits of the *Ziyarah* of his father
(Allah's Messenger).”

I asked, “What should I do if I am in fear and could not enter the
shrine?”

Imam ('a) replied, “Salute from behind the walls.”

**Tradition 6:** Imam Ar-Ridha’ ('a) said: One who performs the
*Ziyarah* of the grave of my father ('a) in Baghdad is like one
performed the *Ziyarah* of Allah's Messenger and that of Amir
Al-Mu’minin ('a), except that Allah's Messenger and Amir Al-Mu’minin
('a) have their own specific ranks.

**Tradition 7:** Imam Jawad ('a) was asked, “What is the reward for the
*Ziyarah* of Allah's Messenger with the sole intention of *Ziyarah*?”

Imam ('a) replied, “It is Paradise and the reward for the *Ziyarah* of
Imam Al-Kadhim ('a) is also same.”

**Tradition 8:** Same as no. 1.

**Tradition 9:** Almost same as no. 6.

**Tradition 10:** A man joined the gathering of Imam Ali Ar-Ridha ('a)
when he was speaking about the excellence of the *Ziyarah* of Imam Musa
Kazim ('a). The newcomer started talking about Baghdad and the
inferiority of its people; about the afflictions, collapsing of earth,
clamour and the bolts of lightning expected to befall the city.

As I (the narrator) got up to leave, I heard Imam Ar-Ridha’ ('a) say,
“As for Imam Al-Kadhim ('a), none will affect him.”

**Tradition 11:** I wrote to Imam Hadi ('a) inquiring about the
*Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) and *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Kadhim and
Imam Jawad ('a).

Imam ('a) replied, “Imam Al-Husayn ('a) is foremost but the *Ziyarah* of
Imam Al-Kadhim and Imam Jawad ('a) is more comprehensive and rewarding.”

**Tradition 12:** Same as no. 7.
