Chapter 77: How Angels Honour The Visitors Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A)
=================================================================

**Tradition 1-3:** Same points as mentioned before.

**Tradition 4:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: When a person sets out to
perform the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a), seven hundred angels
accompany him who will be above, under him, to his right, left, in front
and behind him until he arrives at his destination. After he performs
the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a), an announcer will announce, ''You
have been forgiven; so, continue with good deeds.” These angels will
accompany him back to his house. When they arrive at his house, they
will say, “We leave you in Allah's protection.”

They will visit him continuously till his death. Then they will perform
the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) everyday, the reward of which will
be recorded for this person.

**Tradition 5-10:** Same points as mentioned before.
