Chapter 67: Ziyarah Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A) Is Equivalent To Freeing Of Slaves
=============================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Allah will record the reward
of freeing a thousand slaves for a one who goes to the *Ziyarah* of Imam
Al-Husayn ('a) cognizant of his rights and he is like one who gave a
thousand saddled and bridled horses in the way of Allah.

**Tradition 2:** Same as Chapter 66, Tradition 2.
