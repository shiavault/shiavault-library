Chapter 33: Reward For One Who Recites Couplets About Imam Al-Husayn ('A), Weeps And Makes Others Weep
======================================================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said to me (the narrator), “Abu
Harun, can you recite a couplet for me about Imam Al-Husayn ('a)?” I
recited a couplet for him and he wept. Then he ('a) said, “Recite it
like you usually recite in grief­ stricken tone.” So, I recited: “Pass by
the grave of Husayn and say to his purified bones...” Imam ('a) wept and
asked me to recite more couplets for him. I recited another poem. The
Imam ('a) wept again and I also heard ladies weeping from behind the
curtain. After I finished, the Imam ('a) said: Abu Harun! If one recites
an elegy about Husayn ('a) and weeps and makes ten people weep, Paradise
will become obligatory on him and them. If one recites an elegy on Imam
Al-Husayn ('a) and weeps and makes five people weep, Paradise will
become obligatory on him and them. If one recites an elegy on Imam
Al-Husayn ('a) and weeps and makes one person weep, Paradise will become
obligatory on him and for he that weeps.

One who sheds tears when Imam Al-Husayn ('a) is mentioned before him
will be rewarded by Allah Himself, even if his tears are as small as the
wing of a fly. And Allah will not be pleased with any reward for him
less than Paradise.

**Tradition 2:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said to me (the narrator), “Abu
Ammarah, recite a Mariya of Imam Al-Husayn ('a).” So, I recited a
couplet and he began to weep. Then I recited another couplet and he wept
more. Then I recited another couplet and he wept even more. By Allah, I
continued to recite couplets and he went on crying and I also heard
others crying from inside the house. Then Imam ('a) said: Abu Ammarah,
anyone who recites couplets about Imam Al-Husayn ('a) and makes fifty
people weep will be rewarded with Paradise. Anyone who recites couplets
about Imam Al-Husayn ('a) and makes forty people weep will be rewarded
with Paradise. Anyone who recites couplets about Imam Al-Husayn ('a) and
makes thirty people weep will be rewarded with Paradise. Anyone who
recites couplets about Imam Al-Husayn ('a) and makes twenty people weep
will be rewarded with Paradise. Anyone who recites couplets about Imam
Al-Husayn ('a) and makes ten people weep will be rewarded with Paradise.
Anyone who recites couplets about Imam Al-Husayn ('a) and makes one
person weep will be rewarded with Paradise. Anyone who recites couplets
about Imam Al-Husayn ('a) and weeps will be rewarded with Paradise.
Anyone who recites couplets about Imam Al-Husayn ('a) and tries to weep,
will be rewarded with Paradise.

**Tradition 3:** I (the narrator) went to Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) and recited
an elegy on Imam Al-Husayn ('a). When I reached this verse:

“What a great disaster! Instead of scattering dust over Imam Al-Husayn,
he was rolled in dust!”

A lady weeping behind the curtain, screamed, “Father!”

**Tradition 4:** Similar to no. 2.

**Tradition 5:** Similar to no. 2 and 3.

**Tradition 6:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said, “There is a fixed reward for
every act, except for shedding tears on us.”

**Tradition 7:** Same as no. 4.
