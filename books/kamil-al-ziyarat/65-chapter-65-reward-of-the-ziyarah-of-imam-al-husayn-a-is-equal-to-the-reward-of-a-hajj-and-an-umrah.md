Chapter 65: Reward Of The Ziyarah Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A) Is Equal To The Reward Of A Hajj And An Umrah
======================================================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: “Ziyarah of Imam Al-Husayn
('a) is equal to a Hajj and an Umrah and this much reward.” The narrator
says that Imam ('a) opened and closed his arms thrice.

**Tradition 2:** Same as Chapter 64, no. 3.

**Tradition 3:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) was asked, “What is the reward for
one who performs the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a)?”

Imam ('a) replied, “Allah has appointed four thousand angels on Husayn
('a) who are dishevelled covered with dust, who cry on him till Judgment
Day.”

The narrator asked, “May my parents be sacrificed on you, it is narrated
from your father ('a) that *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) is equal to
a Hajj and an Umrah?”

Imam ('a) replied, “Yes, a Hajj and an Umrah, a Hajj and an Umrah.”

The narrator says that Imam ('a) counted ten Hajj and Umrahs.

**Tradition 4:** Same as no. 1.

**Tradition 5:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Ziyarah of Imam Al-Husayn ('a)
is equivalent to a Hajj and after one has performed obligatory Hajj, it
is equivalent to a Hajj and an Umrah.

**Tradition 6:** Imam Ar-Ridha’ ('a) said: One who performs the
*Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) has performed a Hajj and an Umrah.

I (the narrator) asked, “Does it replace Hajj of Islam?”

Imam ('a) replied: No, it is recorded as Hajj for one who does not have
the means of going to Hajj until the means become available to him to
perform Hajj of the sacred house of Allah. Don't you know that everyday
seventy thousand angels circulate the Ka’aba till nightfall, after which
they ascend back to heavens and are replaced by other angels who
circulate the House until morning?

Indeed, Husayn ('a) is more honourable to Allah than the House. Seventy
thousand angels, dishevelled and covered with dust descend to his grave
at each prayer time and they will not get another opportunity to do so
until Judgment Day.

**Tradition 7:** Same as Chapter 64, no. 3.

**Tradition 8, 9 10:** Same points as in previous traditions.

**Tradition 11, 12, 13:** Same point as mentioned before.

**Tradition 14:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) was asked, “May I be sacrificed on
you! What is the reward for one who performs the *Ziyarah* of Imam
Al-Husayn ('a) and prays two Rakats near his grave?”

Imam ('a) replied, “A Hajj and an Umrah will be written for him.”

I asked, “May I be sacrificed on you! Is it the same for one who goes to
the *Ziyarah* of any Imam whose obedience Allah has made obligatory?”

Imam ('a) replied, “It is same for one who performs the *Ziyarah* of any
Imam whose obedience Allah has made obligatory.” ·

**Tradition 15:** I was with Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) when a group of people
riding donkeys passed by.

Imam ('a) asked, “Where are they going?” I replied, “To the graves of
Uhud Martyrs.”

Imam ('a) asked, “What restrains them from going to the *Ziyarah* of the
lonely martyrs?”

An Iraqi asked, “Is going to his *Ziyarah* obligatory?”

Imam ('a) replied, “His *Ziyarah* is better than performing a Hajj and
an Umrah and another Hajj and Umrah and another Hajj and Umrah and he
continued till he said it is better than twenty Hajj and twenty Umrahs
and then he added it is better than twenty accepted Hajj and Umrahs.”

The narrator says: By Allah·, I did not leave the Imam ('a) until a man
came to him and said, “I have performed Hajj nineteen times. Please pray
for me that Allah bestows me with the twentieth.”

Imam (‘a) asked, “Have you performed the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn
(‘a)?” · ·

The man replied, “No.”

Imam (a’) said, “His *Ziyarah* is better than twenty Hajj.”
