Chapter 25: Killer Of Husayn ('A) And Killer Of Yahya Bin Zakariya ('A)
=======================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) says: The killer of Yahya Ibn
Zakariya ('a) was of illegitimate birth and the killer of Imam Al-Husayn
('a) was also of illegitimate birth. The sky has never wept on anyone
but them.

Allah's Messenger (S) said: There is a position in Hell that no one
deserves, except the killer of Husayn Ibn Ali ('a) and the killer of
Yahya Ibn Zakariya ('a).

**Tradition 3:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) says: By Allah, the descendants of
the killers of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) will be killed for the deeds of their
ancestors.

**Tradition 4:** Same as no. 1.

**Tradition 7:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) was asked, 'Who stopped Fir’awn from
killing Prophet Musa ('a) as mentioned in this verse:

> وَقَالَ فِرْعَوْنُ ذَرُونِي أَقْتُلْ مُوسَىٰ

***“Fir’awn said, ‘Let me kill Musa...’” (40:26).***

Imam ('a) replied, “Because Fir’awn was of illegitimate birth and only
those of illegitimate birth and children of fornicator women can kill
prophets and Divine Proofs.”

**Tradition 8:** Same as no. 6.

**Tradition 9 & 10:** Same as no. 4.

**Tradition 11:** Same as no. 1.
