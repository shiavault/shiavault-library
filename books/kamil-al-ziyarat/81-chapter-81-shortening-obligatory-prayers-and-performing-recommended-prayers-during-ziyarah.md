Chapter 81: Shortening Obligatory Prayers And Performing Recommended Prayers During Ziyarah
===========================================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam Al-Kadhim ('a) was asked about going to the
*Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a). He replied, “I don't want you to omit
it.”

I (the narrator) asked, “What is your opinion about performing
recommended prayers there if I am supposed to shorten prayers?”

Imam ('a) replied, “You can perform as many recommended prayers as you
like in Masjid Al-Haram, Masjid of the Messenger and at the grave of
Imam Al-Husayn ('a). I love (doing) that.”

I asked, “Can I perform recommended prayers at the grave of Imam
Al-Husayn ('a) during the day?”

Imam ('a) replied, “Yes.”

**Tradition 2:** Same as no. 1

**Tradition 3:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) was asked about praying in the holy
sanctuary. Imam ('a) replied, “One should only perform obligatory
prayers in short and he does not have to pray Nafilah.”

**Tradition 4-5:** Same as no. 1.

**Tradition 6:** I (the narrator) asked Imam Al-Kadhim ('a) if a person
who is supposed to shorten obligatory prayers, pray near the grave of
Imam Al-Husayn ('a) in the holy sites and in the two Holy Sanctuaries.

Imam replied: Yes, pray as many optional prayers as you can.
