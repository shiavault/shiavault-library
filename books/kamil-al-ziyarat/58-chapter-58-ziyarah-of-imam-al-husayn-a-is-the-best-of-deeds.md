Chapter 58: Ziyarah Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A) Is The Best Of Deeds
===============================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) was asked about the *Ziyarah* of
Imam Al-Husayn ('a). He replied, “Indeed, it is the best of deeds.”

**Traditions 2 & 3:** Same as no. 1.

**Tradition 4:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: The deed loved most by the
Almighty Allah, is *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) and the best deed
according to Allah is pleasing a believer. And the closest a man can be
to the Almighty Allah, is when he weeps in prostration.

**Traditions 5 & 6:** Same as no. 1.
