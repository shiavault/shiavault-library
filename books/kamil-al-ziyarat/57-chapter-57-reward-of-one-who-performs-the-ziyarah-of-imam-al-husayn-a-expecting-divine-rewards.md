Chapter 57: Reward Of One Who Performs The Ziyarah Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A) Expecting Divine Rewards
==================================================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: One who performs the *Ziyarah*
of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) expecting divine rewards, without arrogance and
ungratefulness and without seeking fame or to show off, will be purified
of his sins just as clothes are purified with water. And no impurity
will remain on them. With every foot he places, a Hajj will be written
for him and every time he raises his foot, an Umrah will be written for
him.

**Tradition 2-3:** Same points as mentioned before.

**Tradition 4:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: One who performs the *Ziyarah*
of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) seeking proximity to Allah, the Great and Mighty,
will be accompanied by Jibra’il, Mikaeel and Israfeel until he returns
home.

**Tradition 5:** Same points as mentioned before.

**Tradition 6:** Zaid Ibn Ali said: Allah will forgive all the sins of
one who performs the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) seeking nothing
but proximity to Almighty Allah, even if they are as great as the foam
of the sea. So, you should go to his *Ziyarah* more; that Allah may
forgive your sins.

**Tradition 7:** Same points as mentioned before.
