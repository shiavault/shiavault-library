Chapter 53: Visitors Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A) Will Enter Paradise Before All
==========================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: “On Judgment Day, visitors of
Imam Al-Husayn ('a) will be given an excellence over others.”

I asked, “What is it?”

Imam ('a) replied, “They will enter Paradise forty years before others;
whereas others have to wait in the accounting process.”
