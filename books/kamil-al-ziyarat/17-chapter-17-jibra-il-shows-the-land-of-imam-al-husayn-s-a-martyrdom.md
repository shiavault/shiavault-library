Chapter 17: Jibra’il Shows The Land Of Imam Al-Husayn's ('A) Martyrdom
======================================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Jibra’il came to Allah's
Messenger (S) while Imam Al-Husayn ('a) was playing before him. Jibra’il
told him that his Ummah would soon slay Husayn ('a).

Allah's Messenger (S) became much aggrieved by this.

Jibra’il said, “Would you like to see the land on which he will be
slain?”

Just then the land between the place where Allah's Messenger (S) sat and
the land on which Imam Al-Husayn ('a) was slain joined with each other.
The Prophet took some dust from there and the earth returned to its
position faster that a blink of an eye. The Prophet left saying:

“Tuba for you, O dust, and Tuba for those who will be slain upon you!”

Imam ('a) added: Companion and Vizier of Sulaiman ('a) also performed
the same feat. That is with the help of the Great Name of Allah, the
earth between Sulaiman's throne and the chair sank within the ground and
the two pieces of land were attached. It seemed as if the chair came out
from under the throne.

Imam ('a) says: Then the earth returned to its original position in less
than a blink of an eye.

**Tradition 2:** Similar to no. 1.

**Traditions 3, 4, 5 and 6:** Same as no. 2.

**Tradition 7:** Ibn Abbas said: The angel who came to Muhammad (S)
informing him about the slaying of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) was the
Trustworthy Spirit, Jibra’il. He came to the Prophet with his wings
spread out, weeping and wailing. He was carrying some dust of Imam
Al-Husayn ('a) from which a musk-like fragrance emanated.

Allah's Messenger (S) asked, 'Will an Ummah that slays my son, achieve
salvation?”

Jibra’il replied, “Almighty Allah will afflict them with disputes and
their hearts will oppose each other.”

**Tradition 8:** Sulaiman (a narrator) asked: Was any angel left in
heavens that did not come to Allah' s Messenger (S) to console him about
his son, Imam Al-Husayn ('a), and to inform him of the reward of Imam
Husai n ('a) with Allah? Was there any angel who did not go to Allah's
Messenger (S) carrying some dust on which Imam:

Husayn ('a) was slain and on which he was abandoned?

Allah's Messenger (S) said, “O Allah, forsake those who forsake Husayn,
kill those who kill him, slaughter those who slaughter him and do not
fulfil their needs.”

Abdul Rahman (another narrator) said: By Allah, the cursed Yazid was
soon taken by Allah for punishment, and he did not have any pleasure
after slaying Imam Al-Husayn ('a). He spent the night drunk and was
found dead and deformed in the morning as if coated with charcoal. All
those who followed Yazid's order of slaying Imam Al-Husayn ('a) and all
who participated in fighting the Imam ('a) lost sanity or were struck
with leprosy which became hereditary in their descendants. Allah's curse
be on them all.

**Tradition 9:** One morning Fatimah (‘a) saw Allah's Messenger (S)
weeping and aggrieved. She asked, “What has upset you, O Allah's
Messenger?” But the Prophet refused to tell her about it. Fatimah (‘a)
said, “I will neither eat nor drink until you tell me.” So, the Prophet
said, “Jibra’il brought me some dust of the land on which a son, who is
not yet conceived, will be slain and this is that dust.” This incident
was of the time when Fatimah (‘a) had not yet conceived Imam Husayn
('a).
