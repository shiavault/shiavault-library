Chapter 80: How To Pray At The Grave Of Imam Al-Husayn ('A)
===========================================================

**Tradition 1:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: Prayers should be performed
besides the head of the grave of Imam Al-Husayn ('a).

**Tradition 2:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) was asked, “When I perform the
*Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a), can I pray to Qibla facing towards
grave?”

Imam ('a) replied, “Move to the side of the grave a little.”

**Tradition 3:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) said: After saluting the martyrs,
return to the grave of Imam Al-Husayn ('a) and face the grave and
perform as many prayers as possible.

**Tradition 4:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) was asked, “How should we pray
besides him when we perform the *Ziyarah* of Imam Al-Husayn ('a)?”

Imam ('a) replied, “Stand behind the grave near his shoulders and invoke
blessings on the Prophet and on Imam Al-Husayn ('a).”

**Tradition 5:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) was asked “Can I pray towards Qibla
facing the grave?”

Imam ('a) replied, “Move to the side of the grave little.”

The man asked, “Can I take some clay from the grave of Imam Al-Husayn
('a) and gain cure from it?”

Imam ('a) replied, “Yes or he said: no problem.”

**Tradition 6:** Imam As-Sadiq (‘a) was asked, “O son of Allah's
Messenger, should one perform the *Ziyarah* of your father \[Imam
Al-Husayn ('a)\]?”

Imam ('a) replied, “Yes; and pray near his grave, but one should not
pray before the grave, on the contrary stand behind it when praying.”
